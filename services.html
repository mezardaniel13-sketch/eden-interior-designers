What We Do

Design services built around experience

Many spaces look good but fail to perform. At Eden, we design spaces that are intentionally planned, emotionally engaging, operationally efficient and environmentally connected.

01 · Hospitality Design

Spaces guests don't just visit — they remember

From the first welcome to the final departure, we design hotels, restaurants, and guest spaces that make people feel comfortable, connected, and inspired to return. We create hotels, restaurants and guest spaces that feel alive, calming and deeply connected to their surroundings. Every detail is intentional — balancing atmosphere, comfort, durability and operational flow.

  • Guest rooms
  • Restaurants & cafés
  • Lobby and lounges
  • Guest-facing commercial spaces
  • Biophilic & eco-conscious interiors
02 · Office & Commercial Design

Workplaces that support wellness and identity

Our designs integrate biophilic principles and environmental awareness, bringing natural elements into corporate settings to improve productivity, wellbeing and spatial experience — spaces that feel open, connected and human-centred.

  • Office space planning & partitioning
  • Biophilic & wellness-driven design
  • Natural materials & green interior concepts
  • Electrical & lighting design
  • Ceiling systems & flooring installations
  • Renovations, refurbishments & painting
  • Real estate fit-outs & show house styling
03 · Residential Design

Homes that reflect personality and intentional living

Every space is carefully developed to balance aesthetics, function and atmosphere. Whether a new build or full renovation, we tailor each detail to enhance flow, usability and long-term liveability.

  • Space planning & layout optimisation
  • Interior concept development & styling
  • Custom finishes, fixtures & fittings
  • Flooring, ceiling & wall treatments
  • Lighting design for ambience & function
  • Renovations & full-home transformations
04 · 3D Visualisation Design

See your space before it's built

Our 3D design process is a core part of Eden's workflow, enabling informed decision-making, design refinement and clarity at every stage. Using advanced rendering techniques, we develop lifelike presentations — true-to-life lighting, shadows and depth — so you can confidently approve designs before construction or installation begins.

05 · Project Management

End-to-end oversight, delivered without compromise

From concept to completion, we manage the entire implementation process — coordinating all site activities, trades and installations to ensure seamless execution and design integrity.

  • End-to-end project coordination
  • Contractor management
  • Quality control & site supervision
  • Design implementation oversight
