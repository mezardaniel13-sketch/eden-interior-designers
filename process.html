How We Work

A tailored process, from first call to final reveal

We provide end-to-end oversight to ensure every design is delivered exactly as envisioned — on time, on budget, and to the highest standard. Here's how a project with Eden unfolds, in four steps.

Step 01

Discovery & Consultation

We start by listening. A site visit and in-depth conversation help us understand your brief, how the space needs to function, your budget, and the story you want it to tell — whether it's a guest room, an office floor, or a family home.

Step 02

Concept & 3D Visualisation

Mood boards, material palettes and photorealistic 3D renders bring your space to life before anything is ordered or built. You'll see layouts, lighting, textures and finishes in realistic detail — and can request changes with full clarity and precision.

Step 03

Sourcing & Design Development

Once the concept is approved, we finalise specifications, materials and finishes, and begin coordinating contractors and suppliers — refining and evolving details beyond the initial brief as the design is developed for execution.

Step 04

Execution & Handover

We manage the entire implementation on-site — coordinating trades and installations, running quality control, and protecting your investment — ending in a full styling pass and final reveal of your completed space.

Why It Works

Every step is guided by the same five principles

Experience

How people feel in a space

Function

How spaces perform in real life

Identity

How a space represents a brand

Environment

Integration of natural elements

Detail

Precision in materials & execution
